About Me

In June 2025, I graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Electronics from Kristu Jayanti University, Bangalore, India. The program combined two majors: Computer Science and Electronics. It covered programming, databases, and data analysis, alongside digital electronics and embedded systems. I also gained practical experience in microcontroller hardware interfacing with peripheral devices.

As part of my degree, I completed a three-month Data Analysis internship at Unified Mentor from September to December 2024. My work involved data preprocessing, data cleaning, and exploratory data analysis (EDA) using Python. I contributed to improving data quality and reliability, ensuring datasets were ready for Business Intelligence analysis and reporting.

Since September 2025, I have been pursuing an MSc in Control and Robotics at Centrale Nantes. I am currently in the final year of the Data Science, Signal and Image Processing track. The program focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and numerical optimization methods applied to data analysis and image processing.

In parallel, I carried out AI Response Evaluation assignments as a freelancer for RWS TrainAI from August to October 2026. My work involved assessing AI-generated responses against predefined quality criteria, including relevance, accuracy, completeness, and instruction adherence. I also compared outputs across different models and documented evaluation results to support continuous model improvement.

I am seeking a six-month final-year internship starting in February 2027 in Data Science. I have a particular interest in computer vision and image processing, while remaining open to broader data analysis applications, especially in industrial environments. I am eager to join a technical team where I can contribute to real-world projects, apply my knowledge, and continue developing my skills alongside experienced professionals.
